Derivative Rules

Exponential, trigonometric, and implicit differentiation

Exponential Derivatives

Natural base\(\dfrac{d}{dx}(e^x)=e^x\)
General base\(\dfrac{d}{dx}(a^x)=a^x\ln(a),\quad a>0,\;a\ne1\)
Natural base composite\(\dfrac{d}{dx}(e^{g(x)})=g'(x)e^{g(x)}\)
General base composite\(\dfrac{d}{dx}(a^{g(x)})=g'(x)a^{g(x)}\ln(a)\)
Natural logarithm\(\dfrac{d}{dx}\ln(u)=\dfrac{u'}{u}\)
Inverse tangent\(\dfrac{d}{dx}\arctan(u)=\dfrac{u'}{1+u^2}\)
Sine\(\dfrac{d}{dx}\sin(u)=\cos(u)\,u'\)
Tangent\(\dfrac{d}{dx}\tan(u)=\sec^2(u)\,u'\)

Linearity and Powers

Constant multiple\(\dfrac{d}{dx}[cf(x)]=cf'(x)\)
Sum or difference\(\dfrac{d}{dx}[f(x)\pm g(x)]=f'(x)\pm g'(x)\)
Power rule\(\dfrac{d}{dx}(x^n)=nx^{n-1}\)

Chain Rule

Composite function\(\dfrac{d}{dx}f(g(x))=f'(g(x))g'(x)\)
Linear inner function\(u=ax+b\;\Longrightarrow\;u'=a\)

Product Rule

Two functions\((uv)'=u'v+uv'\)
Example form\(\dfrac{d}{dx}[x f(x)]=f(x)+xf'(x)\)

Quotient Rule

Two functions\(\left(\dfrac{u}{v}\right)'=\dfrac{vu'-uv'}{v^2}\)

Tangent Lines

Point-slope form\(y-y_1=m(x-x_1),\quad m=\left.\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right|_{x=x_1}\)

Normal Lines

Perpendicular gradient\(m_n=-\dfrac{1}{m_t}\)
Equation of a normal\(y-y_1=m_n(x-x_1)\)
Horizontal tangent\(m_t=0\;\Longrightarrow\;\text{normal is vertical: }x=x_1\)

Power and Product Rules

General power\(\dfrac{d}{dx}[f(x)]^n=n[f(x)]^{n-1}f'(x)\)
Product rule\((uv)'=u'v+uv'\)

Curve Analysis

Stationary point\(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=0\)
Second derivative test\(y''>0:\text{ minimum},\quad y''<0:\text{ maximum}\)
Normal gradient\(m_n=-\dfrac{1}{m_t}\)

Turning Points – Maximum and Minimum

Find a stationary point\(y'(a)=0\)
Local minimum\(y'(a)=0,\quad y''(a)>0\)
Local maximum\(y'(a)=0,\quad y''(a)<0\)
Inconclusive second derivative\(y''(a)=0\;\Longrightarrow\;\text{use another test}\)
Coordinates\((a,f(a))\)
Reciprocal powers\(\dfrac{1}{x}=x^{-1},\quad\dfrac{d}{dx}(x^{-1})=-x^{-2},\quad x\ne0\)
Solve completely\(\text{Find every real root of }y'=0\text{ within the domain.}\)
Stationary versus turning point\(y'(a)=0\text{ does not always imply a maximum or minimum.}\)

Implicit Differentiation

General relation\(F(x,y)=c\;\Longrightarrow\;\dfrac{d}{dx}F(x,y)=0\)
Exponential of y\(\dfrac{d}{dx}(e^{g(y)})=e^{g(y)}g'(y)\dfrac{dy}{dx}\)
