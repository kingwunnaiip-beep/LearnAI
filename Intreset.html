Simple interest and amount

Interest: I = Prt. Final amount: A = P(1 + rt). Use r as a decimal and t in years. Simple interest is always calculated on the original principal.

Compound interest and compounding periods

Final amount: A = P(1 + j/m)mt, where j is nominal annual rate, m is compounds per year, and t is years. Effective annual rate: (1 + j/m)m − 1.

Present value, loan payments, and remaining balance

Present value: PV = FV(1+i)n. Equal loan payment: PMT = PV·i1 − (1+i)−n. After k payments, remaining balance = PMT·1 − (1+i)−(n−k)i.

Regular deposits, tax, inflation, and changing rates

End-of-period deposits: FV = PMT·(1+i)n−1i. Tax on interest: net amount = P + (A−P)(1−tax rate). Today’s purchasing power: after-tax amount(1+inflation rate)years. For changing rates, multiply each period’s growth factors.
