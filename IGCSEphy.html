Question 1: Distance-Time Graph Interpretation 0625/22/F/M/25 Q2

The distance-time graph shows the journey of a cyclist moving along a straight road.

Which row in the table correctly describes the motion of the cyclist during section Q and section R?

  • A: Section Q: Stationary | Section R: Moving at a constant speed of 10 m/s
  • B: Section Q: Moving at a constant speed of 3.3 m/s | Section R: Moving at a constant speed of 10 m/s
  • C: Section Q: Stationary | Section R: Accelerating at 10 m/s²
  • D: Section Q: Stationary | Section R: Moving at a constant speed of 6.0 m/s
Correct Answer: A
Detailed Explanation:
  • Section Q (from 10 s to 30 s): The distance remains constant at 100 m. A horizontal line on a distance-time graph indicates zero speed (the cyclist is stationary).
  • Section R (from 30 s to 50 s): The distance increases steadily from 100 m to 300 m over a time interval of 50 - 30 = 20 s. The gradient of a distance-time graph represents speed:
    Speed = Change in distance / Change in time = (300 m - 100 m) / (50 s - 30 s) = 200 m / 20 s = 10 m/s
Question 2: Speed-Time Graph & Distance Calculation 0625/21/M/J/25 Q3

A car travels along a straight road. The speed-time graph for the motion of the car is shown below.

What is the total distance travelled by the car during the 14.0 seconds?

  • A: 105 m
  • B: 165 m
  • C: 210 m
  • D: 120 m
Correct Answer: B
Detailed Explanation:
  • The total distance travelled is equal to the area under the speed-time graph.
  • The area is composed of a triangle from 0 s to 6.0 s and a rectangle from 6.0 s to 14.0 s:
    Area of Triangle = 0.5 * base * height = 0.5 * 6.0 s * 15 m/s = 45 m
    Area of Rectangle = base * height = (14.0 s - 6.0 s) * 15 m/s = 8.0 s * 15 m/s = 120 m
    Total Distance = 45 m + 120 m = 165 m
Question 3: Acceleration Calculation & Distance 0625/23/M/J/25 Q2

A high-speed train accelerates uniformly from rest to a velocity of 54 m/s in a time of 18 seconds.

What is the acceleration of the train and what distance does it travel during this time?

  • A: Acceleration = 3.0 m/s² | Distance = 486 m
  • B: Acceleration = 3.0 m/s² | Distance = 972 m
  • C: Acceleration = 0.33 m/s² | Distance = 486 m
  • D: Acceleration = 3.0 m/s² | Distance = 27 m
Correct Answer: A
Detailed Explanation:
  • 1. Acceleration (a):
    a = (v - u) / t = (54 m/s - 0 m/s) / 18 s = 3.0 m/s²
  • 2. Distance (s):
    Average Speed = (0 + 54) / 2 = 27 m/s
    Distance = Average Speed * Time = 27 m/s * 18 s = 486 m
Question 4: Non-Uniform Acceleration Graph 0625/22/O/N/25 Q3

The speed-time graph below represents the motion of an object falling through the atmosphere.

Which statement correctly describes the motion of the object between points W and Y?

  • B: The speed is increasing and the acceleration is decreasing.
  • C: The speed is constant and the acceleration is zero.
  • D: The speed is increasing and the acceleration is constant.
Correct Answer: B

Question 5: Average Speed of Multi-Stage Journey 0625/21/F/M/24 Q2

A car travels along a straight road. It moves at a constant speed of 20 m/s for 100 seconds, stops at traffic lights for 20 seconds, and then travels a further distance of 1200 m in 80 seconds.

What is the average speed of the car for the entire journey?

  • A: 16.0 m/s
  • B: 17.8 m/s
  • C: 20.0 m/s
  • D: 15.0 m/s
Correct Answer: A
Detailed Explanation:
  • Step 1: Calculate total distance:
    Stage 1 distance = 20 m/s * 100 s = 2000 m
    Stage 2 distance = 0 m
    Stage 3 distance = 1200 m
    Total distance = 2000 m + 0 m + 1200 m = 3200 m
  • Step 2: Calculate total time:
    Total time = 100 s + 20 s + 80 s = 200 s
  • Step 3: Calculate average speed:
    Average speed = 3200 m / 200 s = 16.0 m/s
