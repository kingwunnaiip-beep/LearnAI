Trigonometric Differentiation

Angles in radians

Basic Trigonometric Derivatives

Sine\(\dfrac{d}{dx}(\sin x)=\cos x\)
Cosine\(\dfrac{d}{dx}(\cos x)=-\sin x\)
Tangent\(\dfrac{d}{dx}(\tan x)=\sec^2x\)

Linearity and Powers

Constant multiple\(\dfrac{d}{dx}[cf(x)]=cf'(x)\)
Sum or difference\(\dfrac{d}{dx}[f(x)\pm g(x)]=f'(x)\pm g'(x)\)
Power rule\(\dfrac{d}{dx}(x^n)=nx^{n-1}\)

Chain Rule

Sine of a function\(\dfrac{d}{dx}[\sin u]=\cos u\,u'\)
Cosine of a function\(\dfrac{d}{dx}[\cos u]=-\sin u\,u'\)
Tangent of a function\(\dfrac{d}{dx}[\tan u]=\sec^2u\,u'\)
Linear inner function\(u=ax+b\;\Longrightarrow\;u'=a\)

Product Rule

Two functions\((uv)'=u'v+uv'\)
Example form\(\dfrac{d}{dx}[x f(x)]=f(x)+xf'(x)\)

Quotient Rule

Two functions\(\left(\dfrac{u}{v}\right)'=\dfrac{vu'-uv'}{v^2}\)

Powers of Trigonometric Functions

Sine power\(\dfrac{d}{dx}[\sin^n u]=n\sin^{n-1}u\cos u\,u'\)
Cosine power\(\dfrac{d}{dx}[\cos^n u]=-n\cos^{n-1}u\sin u\,u'\)
General power\(\dfrac{d}{dx}[f(x)]^n=n[f(x)]^{n-1}f'(x)\)
